Strict Verbatim Standard (Core Platform Promise)

Nothing added that is not in the case. Do not generate hypothetical litigation strategies, counsel tactics, or speculative arguments. All facts, trial propositions, statutory quotes, holdings, precedents, and citations must trace directly to the certified judgment or official law report metadata.

2 Story Episodes & Depth Layers

Cases are broken down into sequential episodes (dynamic count, typically 8 episodes). Each episode requires three synchronized layers:

Layer A: General Story Narrative

Factual, engaging storytelling for general readers. Must include Episode Title, Kicker (e.g., EPISODE 01 · THE PREMISE), and 1–2 paragraphs describing the facts and timeline.

Layer B: Student Depth (Academic & Exam Focus)

Legal Analysis: 1 concise paragraph highlighting doctrinal significance.
Ratio Decidendi: Clear binding legal rule.
Exam Angle: Relevance for CLAT-PG, Judiciary Mains, and AIBE.

Layer C: Advocate Depth (Clean Trial Propositions)

TRIAL PROPOSITION: Exactly one clean sentence naming the legal point that this episode's facts or reasoning illustrate.
Example: TRIAL PROPOSITION: Section 66A of the IT Act creates a chilling effect on legitimate free speech and is void for vagueness.
Paragraph Pinpoints: Precise paragraph numbers (e.g., ¶8, ¶104) quoting the specific rule.

3 The 5-Part Consolidated Advocate Reference Block

Appears once at the end of the case dossier (after the final episode) when toggled to Advocate Depth. Contains 5 authoritative sections:

Part 1 · Statutory Text As Reproduced In The Judgment

Quote verbatim any section(s) of law the judgment reproduces in full. Attribute exactly to the Act and Section.

{ "act": "Information Technology Act, 2000", "section": "s. 66A", "quotedText": ""66A. Punishment for sending offensive messages through communication service, etc.—Any person who sends, by means of a computer resource or a communication device..."", "pinpoint": "SCR Page 12, Para 4" }

Part 2 · The Court's Enumerated Holdings

Numbered list of operative legal conclusions with precise pinpoint citations (SCR/SCC page & column or paragraph numbers).

{ "number": "1", "holding": "Section 66A of the Information Technology Act is unconstitutional and violates Article 19(1)(a).", "pinpoint": "¶111, SCC p. 64" }

Part 3 · Precedents Discussed In This Judgment

Table of authorities cited inside the judgment with the court's verbatim treatment verb (Affirmed, Applied, Followed, Referred to, Distinguished, Doubted, Overruled).

{ "caseName": "Romesh Thappar v. State of Madras", "citation": "[1950] SCR 594", "treatment": "Followed", "proposition": "On clear and present danger threshold for public order restrictions" }

Part 4 · Subsequent Citator History

Citator metadata entries recorded in the law report with standard codes (F, RF, D, O) plus the mandatory disclaimer.

Mandatory Citator Notice: "These are the citator entries recorded in this report and may not be complete or current. Verify this case's present status through a live citator (SCC Online, Manupatra, or equivalent) before relying on it in an active matter."

Part 5 · Full Parallel Citation Index

Comprehensive list of parallel reporter citations for cross-referencing in any court.

"parallelCitations": [ "AIR 2015 SC 1523", "(2015) 5 SCC 1", "(2015) 2 SCR 1", "2015 Cri LJ 2001", "2015 (2) SCALE 1" ]
